About the studio

A premium tattoo experience in the heart of Koper.

Located at Prešernov trg 5, Apollo Tattoo Studio combines detailed tattoo work, individual consultation and a calm studio atmosphere. The focus is simple: create tattoos that look powerful, personal and technically clean.

Designed for clients who want more than a random tattoo.

Every tattoo starts with a conversation. The idea, placement, size and reference images are used to build a design that fits the client’s body and personal style.

From delicate small tattoos to complex realistic compositions, Apollo focuses on careful planning, strong contrast, clean detail and professional hygiene standards throughout the entire process.

The process

From your idea to a finished tattoo.

A simple step-by-step process makes it easier for new clients to understand how booking works and what they should prepare before contacting the studio.

1

Send your idea

Share your concept, reference photos, preferred placement and approximate size. The more context you give, the easier it is to plan the right design.

2

Consultation

The idea is reviewed from both an artistic and technical perspective, including placement, readability, size and long-term visual impact.

3

Custom design

The tattoo concept is refined into a design that fits your body, style and the meaning behind the tattoo.

4

Tattoo session

Your session takes place in a clean, professional and welcoming environment, with attention to detail from start to finish.

5

Aftercare

After the session, you receive clear aftercare guidance so your tattoo can heal properly and keep its detail.
